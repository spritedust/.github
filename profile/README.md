Solo dev making offline desktop apps that are tiny by design, hackable, and expressive.

I like to imagine a world where computers are good for people, good for the planet, and still feel a little bit magical. Computers should be fun and helpful; not something that constantly asks for your data, demands enormous data centers, or puts too much control in the hands of a few people.

So I make small software that offers another option. The software I make is tiny by design: no more features than necessary, colorful, and hopefully a joy to use.

Source code is available on GitHub. Free to download, contribute to, hack away.

Ready-to-install packages are available on Itch.io for a small price if you'd like to support development.

Choice is yours!

You can also follow the Telegram channel for news, updates, and a more direct look at what I'm making.
